GEH SERBIA / COMPANY

Industrial experience. Local responsibility.

TE-TO Pančevo uses natural gas in a combined cycle to generate electricity and process steam. The steam is intended for the Pančevo Oil Refinery, while electricity is supplied to the Serbian power market.

01 / STRUCTURE

Two related companies

Gazprom Energoholding Serbia LLC was established in 2015. Its wholly owned subsidiary Gazprom Energoholding Serbia TE-TO Pančevo LLC was formed for construction management and subsequent plant operations.
