GEH SERBIA / PANČEVO CCPP

Energy through a connected cycle.

TE-TO Pančevo generates electricity and process steam through a natural-gas combined cycle. The plant serves industrial demand and Serbia’s electricity market.

GEH / PANČEVO CCPP
GEH / CONTEXT

How the systems work together

Gas turbines generate electricity. Exhaust heat is recovered in heat-recovery boilers, and the resulting steam serves the steam cycle and industrial processes.

How energy is produced

Four stages of the combined cycle.

01

Natural gas

Natural gas is the primary fuel for the plant.

02

Gas turbine

Combustion in a gas turbine drives an electricity generator.

03

Heat-recovery boiler

Remaining exhaust heat is used to produce steam.

04

Steam turbine

Steam drives a turbine and provides process heat to industry.

GEH / VERIFIED INFORMATION

Two turbines. Two stages of energy recovery.

Natural gas drives the gas turbines. Heat in their exhaust gases is then recovered in heat-recovery steam generators. Steam powers a separate turbine, producing additional electricity.
