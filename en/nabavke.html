Contractor HSE qualification requires MP0400-OB028 and MP0400-OB030. Documentation is submitted electronically in Serbian, using mutually accepted encryption. The official procedure takes precedence.

MP0400 / HSE

HSE contractor qualification

Contractors and subcontractors must generally complete HSE qualification before participating in procurement, subject to the published exemptions. Effective 1 January 2025.

01Complete MP0400-OB028 and MP0400-OB030 with the required HSE evidence.
02Submit documents electronically and in the Serbian language.
03Encrypt the ZIP package; unprotected documents will not be reviewed.
04Send to hse@geh-serbia.rs and inform employees about personal-data processing.

Categories exempt from the HSE qualification requirement
  • Services performed solely at the contractor’s site.
  • Specified administrative and professional services without excluded site activities.
  • Healthcare services.
  • Suppliers and supply-contract counterparties treated as third parties.
